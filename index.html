Chain Solutions

Naho Trading is a dedicated scrap metal trading company connecting global suppliers with steel mills, foundries, and smelters across international markets. We specialise in sourcing, grading, and exporting high-quality ferrous and non-ferrous scrap ensuring every tonne we ship meets the strict specifications our customers depend on.

From the moment material is sourced to the day it arrives at your facility, Naho Trading manages the entire supply chain with precision. Our team brings deep commodity expertise, trusted supplier networks, and a commitment to transparent, ethical business practices that our partners count on. Whether you need a consistent long-term supply partner or a reliable source for a single bulk order, we have the knowledge, network, and execution capability to deliver.

Our Process

How We Work

Naho Trading is built to make international scrap metal sourcing simple, reliable, and worry-free.

Step 1:

Enquire

- Tell us what grade, quantity, and specification you need. Our team responds fast.

Step 2:

We Source

- We tap our trusted supplier network to find material that meets your exact requirements.

Step 3:

Inspection

- Every shipment is inspected prior to loading. Quality is verified, not assumed.

Step 4:

Documentation & Finance

- We handle all trade documentation, letters of credit, and banking instruments.

Step 5:

Shipment

- Material is loaded and dispatched via containerised sea freight with reliable global carriers.

Step 6:

Delivery

- Your material arrives to specification, on time, ready to process.
